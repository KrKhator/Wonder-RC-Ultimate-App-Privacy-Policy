Privacy Policy for Wonder Rock Ultimate RC

Effective Date: November 8, 2026

Wonder Rock ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application, Wonder Rock Ultimate RC (Package Name: com.Wonderrock.ultimateRC), handles device permissions and user data.

1. Overview

Wonder Rock Ultimate RC is a control application designed strictly to connect to and control physical Wonder Rock RC toy vehicles via Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE). We do NOT collect, store, share, or transmit any personal data, location data, or device identifiers.

2. Device Permissions Used

To function properly, the App requests the following permissions on your Android device:

  • Bluetooth Permissions (BLUETOOTH_SCAN, BLUETOOTH_CONNECT, BLUETOOTH_ADMIN): Used solely to scan for, connect to, and transmit control commands (steering, throttle, spray features) to your physical RC vehicle over Bluetooth Low Energy.
  • Location Permission (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION): Required by the Android OS on Android 11 and lower to discover nearby Bluetooth LE devices. We do NOT collect, track, or record your location.
  • Accelerometer / Motion Sensors: Used locally on your device for optional phone tilt steering mode. Sensor data is processed in real-time on your device and is never saved or transmitted.

3. Data Collection and Transmission

  • Personal Information: We collect NO personal details (names, emails, phone numbers, or account credentials).
  • Location Data: We do NOT collect or log your GPS or location data.
  • Analytics & Ads: We do NOT collect device identifiers or track app usage.
  • Offline Functionality: The app operates offline and does not transfer any data over the Internet.

4. Third-Party Services

The App does NOT use any third-party SDKs, ad networks, or analytics services (such as Firebase, AdMob, or Google Analytics).

5. Children's Privacy

Our app is safe for users of all ages, including children under 13. Because no personal data is collected, the app complies fully with COPPA and GDPR regulations.
